Projection Screens

Overview

Professional Projection Screens

Commercial-grade projection screens designed for maximum image contrast, wide viewing angles, and long-lasting durability in educational institutions.

EYESOFT supplies motorized electric screens, manual pull-down screens, tripod portable screens, and fixed frame auditorium screens in sizes ranging from 84 inches to 300 inches.

catalogue Reference: Commercial Hardware Lineup • Authentic EYESOFT Integration

Key Features & Capabilities

  • Matte white high-gain multi-layer fabric for crystal-clear image reproduction
  • Wide 160-degree viewing angle so every student in the room sees perfectly
  • Heavy-duty tubular motors with thermal overload protection for motorized screens
  • Black borders for enhanced perceived contrast and sharp frame focus
  • Ceiling recessed and wall surface mounting brackets included
