Comprehensive Digital Signage & Seamless LED Video Wall Networks

EYESOFT specializes in enterprise visual communication and ultra-high-resolution display systems. From interactive standalone digital signage totems and centralized cloud content broadcasting networks to bezel-less micro-LED video walls and multi-window command center control matrices, we supply, install, calibrate, and support hardware with continuous 24/7 operational reliability.

Commercial Signage & Kiosks

Digital Signage & Interactive Information Kiosks

Deliver dynamic promotional campaigns, campus announcements, wayfinding, and live real-time multimedia feeds across multiple locations from one centralized dashboard.

  • High-Brightness Commercial Panels: 700 to 2500 nits sunlight-readable commercial displays with anti-glare coatings
  • Cloud-Managed Content Management (CMS): Drag-and-drop playlist scheduling, split-screen zones, and remote player monitoring
  • Interactive Touch Kiosks: Floor-standing totems with 10-point capacitive touch for wayfinding, product browsing, and ticketing
  • Industrial 24/7 Chassis: Heavy-gauge metal enclosures with tamper-proof security locks and integrated cooling fans
Fine-Pitch Video Walls

Seamless LED Video Walls & Command Centers

Precision engineered direct-view LED walls and ultra-narrow bezel LCD matrices designed for mission-critical command centers, boardrooms, and public auditoriums.

  • Zero-Bezel Direct-View LED: Ultra-fine pixel pitches from P0.9 to P2.5 delivering perfectly seamless canvases of any aspect ratio
  • Hardware Video Processors: Multi-window picture-in-picture (PiP), multi-source 4K60 scaling, and IP streaming camera decode
  • Front-Service Magnetic Modules: Easy individual tile replacement from the front without disassembling the wall structure
  • Redundant Power & Data: Dual power supplies and loop-through signal cabling preventing blackouts during critical events
