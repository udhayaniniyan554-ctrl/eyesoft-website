SOLUTION OVERVIEW

Digital Classroom Systems

Interactive laser projectors, ceramic steel whiteboards, and digital teacher podiums for modern classrooms.

The 21st-century classroom has evolved beyond chalkboards into an interactive, multi-sensory learning hub. EYESOFT's Digital Classroom Systems integrate ultra-short throw interactive laser projectors with magnetic ceramic steel projection boards, multimedia teacher podiums, and integrated classroom audio. Educators can write, draw, annotate digital textbooks, display animations, and save lesson notes, creating deeply engaging learning experiences for students.

ENGINEERED ADVANTAGES

Key System Features

High-performance audio-visual engineering standards integrated into every Digital Classroom Systems installation.

Ultra-Short Throw Laser Projection

Mounts directly above the board eliminating presenter glare and distracting teacher shadows completely.

Magnetic Ceramic Steel Surface

Virtually indestructible, scratch-proof surface supporting both regular dry-erase marker writing and interactive digital pens.

Integrated Teacher Rostrum

Ergonomic electronic podium housing a mini PC, document visualizer, touch tablet, and central amplifier.

Lesson Recording Software

One-click lesson recording software enabling teachers to record speech and board annotations for absent students.

TECHNICAL CAPABILITIES

Architectural & Practical Benefits

•

Zero Lamp Replacement Costs

Solid-state laser engine delivers 20,000+ hours of maintenance-free operation with no filters or lamps to replace.

•

Instant On/Off Operation

Laser light source turns on to full brightness in 5 seconds, saving valuable teaching time in every class period.

Eye-Care Certified Low Blue Light

Specially coated anti-reflective surface prevents eye strain for students seated across all angles of the room.

Turnkey School Implementation
