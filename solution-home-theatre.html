SOLUTIONS OVERVIEW

Cinema-Grade Entertainment In Your Private Residence

Turnkey home cinema engineering, 4K HDR laser projection, Dolby Atmos spatial audio, and bespoke acoustic calibration.

EYESOFT designs and builds world-class private screening rooms and luxury residential home cinemas. We coordinate structural acoustics, noise isolation, speaker baffle walls, custom seating tiers, and centralized smart control. Every installation is tailored to the physical dimensions of your space, delivering pristine cinematic visuals and spine-tingling multi-dimensional audio that rivals commercial cinemas.

ENGINEERED ADVANTAGES

Key System Capabilities

Uncompromising audio-visual fidelity integrated into every EYESOFT private home cinema installation.

4K HDR Laser Projection

True 4K UHD laser optical engines producing infinite contrast, DCI-P3 wide color gamut, and HDR10+ dynamic tone mapping.

Dolby Atmos & DTS:X Spatial Audio

Discrete 7.2.4 to 9.4.6 multi-channel immersive surround sound with hidden in-wall speakers and high-excursion subwoofers.

Acoustically Transparent Screens

Micro-perforated and woven motorized tensioned screens that allow left, center, and right speakers to project directly from behind the image.

Architectural Acoustic Treatment

Custom Fabri-Trak acoustic wall fabric systems, corner bass traps, and 2D sound diffusers eliminating room reverberation.
