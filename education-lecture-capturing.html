SOLUTION OVERVIEW

Lecture Capturing And Broadcasting

Automated professor tracking cameras, high-lumen auditorium projection, and live streaming to university LMS.

Higher education institutions require flexible architectures that bridge classroom teaching with remote online learning. EYESOFT's Lecture Capturing and Broadcasting solutions utilize intelligent AI tracking PTZ cameras, studio-grade ceiling microphone arrays, and hardware recording appliances. The system automatically records the professor, whiteboard annotations, and presentation slides, cataloging classes directly into the university's LMS (Moodle, Blackboard, Canvas).

ENGINEERED ADVANTAGES

Key System Features

High-performance audio-visual engineering standards integrated into every Lecture Capturing And Broadcasting installation.

AI Professor Auto-Tracking

Intelligent PTZ cameras that follow the instructor smoothly across the stage without requiring camera operators or sensor lanyards.

Multi-Channel Synchronized Ingest

Simultaneously captures professor video, audio, and presentation screen in full 1080p/4K resolution.

One-Touch Rostrum Recording

Lecturers start and stop recording with a single button tap on the digital podium, with zero IT intervention required.

Seamless University LMS Integration

Automated uploading and cataloging directly into Moodle, Canvas, and Blackboard student portals.
