Campus Security Surveillance

High-resolution IP surveillance, perimeter security, school bus camera tracking, and emergency SOS stations.

Ensuring student and faculty safety is the paramount responsibility of every educational institution. EYESOFT delivers campus-wide security surveillance integrating high-resolution vandal-resistant dome cameras in corridors, weatherproof night-vision bullet cameras along perimeters, school bus GPS video tracking, and a centralized security command room. Our proactive AI alerts detect unauthorized intrusions and perimeter breaches before incidents occur.

ENGINEERED ADVANTAGES

Key System Features

High-performance audio-visual engineering standards integrated into every Campus Security Surveillance installation.

High-Density Hallway Coverage

Wide-angle vandal-proof dome cameras providing complete coverage of hallways, stairwells, and entry foyers.

Perimeter Intrusion Detection

AI line-crossing detection instantly alerting security guards if anyone climbs campus perimeter walls after hours.

School Bus Mobile Surveillance

Vibration-proof mobile NVRs with dual cameras and GPS tracking ensuring pupil safety during transit.

Emergency SOS Help Points

Wall-mounted two-way emergency intercom stations in parking lots and remote sports grounds connecting directly to security.
