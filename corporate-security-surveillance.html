SOLUTION OVERVIEW

Surveillance & Security Solutions

High-definition IP surveillance, smart AI perimeter detection, and integrated biometric access control.

Safeguarding physical corporate assets, intellectual property, and personnel requires enterprise-grade security intelligence. EYESOFT designs comprehensive IP surveillance ecosystems incorporating 4K vandal-proof dome cameras, long-range night vision bullet cameras, AI perimeter intrusion detection, and centralized network video recorders (NVR). Our security systems integrate seamlessly with biometric turnstiles and security control rooms for 360-degree facility protection.

ENGINEERED ADVANTAGES

Key System Features

High-performance audio-visual engineering standards integrated into every Surveillance & Security Solutions installation.

4K Ultra-HD Optical Surveillance

Crystal-clear facial and license plate detail capture in high-risk zones, parking complexes, and building entry points.

AI Video Analytics & Alerts

Real-time automated detection of perimeter line crossing, loitering, unattended baggage, and prohibited zone entry.

RAID-5/6 multi-terabyte storage configurations ensuring 30 to 90 days of continuous recording with zero data loss.
